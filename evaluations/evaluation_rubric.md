# SCRIPTXT Human Evaluation Rubric

Evaluate each generated campaign (LinkedIn post, X thread, Instagram caption, blog outline, and content calendar) on a **1–5 scale** across the seven criteria below.

## Scoring Scale

- **1 — Poor**: Off-topic, generic, unsafe, or unusable without a complete rewrite.
- **2 — Weak**: Partially relevant but misses audience nuances, tone, or platform constraints.
- **3 — Acceptable**: Solid draft that meets core requirements with light editing needed.
- **4 — Strong**: Specific, well-structured, platform-native copy ready with minimal tweaks.
- **5 — Publish-Ready**: Crisp, original, audience-specific, and strictly compliant with all guardrails.

---

## Evaluation Dimensions (1–5)

| Dimension | What to Look For | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Acceptable) | 5 (Publish-Ready) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Relevance to Topic** | Does every asset stay focused on the user's topic without drifting into filler? | Ignores topic or drifts into generic platitudes. | Covers topic accurately with minor filler. | Laser-focused on topic across all channels and calendar days. |
| **2. Audience Fit** | Are examples, vocabulary, and pain points tailored to the stated audience? | Generic "everyone" copy. | Mentions audience but uses broad advice. | Reflects real workflows and priorities of the target audience. |
| **3. Tone Match** | Does the writing consistently reflect the selected tone and optional brand voice? | Clashes with requested tone. | Mostly matches tone with occasional slips. | Unmistakably captures requested tone and brand voice. |
| **4. Originality** | Are hooks varied across calendar days and free of clichés? | Repeated hooks or stale templates. | Mostly distinct hooks with minor repetition. | Distinct, memorable hooks across every channel and day. |
| **5. Platform Fit** | Does each output respect platform conventions (LinkedIn 120–180w, X 3–7 short posts, Instagram <2,200 chars + alt text, Blog H2 sections)? | Wrong format or length violations. | Minor formatting tweaks needed. | Native pacing, formatting, hashtags, and length on every platform. |
| **6. CTA Clarity** | Is the call to action clear, natural, and aligned with the campaign goal? | Missing or confusing CTA. | Generic CTA present. | Specific, compelling CTA integrated cleanly in each piece. |
| **7. Factual Safety** | Are outputs free of fabricated statistics, unsupported absolute claims, PII, and impersonation? | Contains invented stats, absolute claims, or PII. | No unsafe claims; minor ambiguity. | Strictly grounded; any unverified claims flagged or avoided. |

---

## Automated Threshold Targets

- **Schema pass rate**: 100%
- **Length-limit pass rate**: >= 95%
- **Duplicate-hook failure rate**: 0%
- **Guardrail violation rate**: 0%
